# The bank and .syx import, end to end: a bank arrives, a voice arrives, a voice is saved into a
# slot, a damaged bank is refused, and the host asks for both back. tests/syxcheck.py then reads the
# WAV and the files the run left behind and checks that what came out is what went in.
#
#   tests/run_host.sh tests/bank.txt     (it runs tests/mksyx.py first to make the .syx files)
#
# Timings matter: the chords are struck fresh each time so that two windows can be compared sample
# for sample, which is the only way to prove that saving and reloading a voice loses nothing.

# 1. a 32-voice bank arrives over USB-MIDI and is written to flash
300 syx build/host/bank.syx
600 shot

# 2. the voice knob runs past the 20 factory voices into slot 1, TEST 01: a sine at x1
800 enc 6 20
1000 shot
1000 keys 0x4880
1900 keys 0

# 3. a single voice arrives: it lands in the editor, as a DX7 does, marked as an edit
2100 syx build/host/voice.syx
2400 shot
2500 keys 0x4880
3400 keys 0

# 4. save it: press, wind to slot 5, press again
3600 btn 9
3700 shot
3800 enc 6 4
3900 shot
4000 btn 9
4300 shot

# 5. leave the slot and come back to it: the name is now the voice that was saved
4500 enc 6 -1
4700 enc 6 1
4900 shot
5000 keys 0x4880
5900 keys 0

# 6. a bank with one bit wrong in its checksum: refused, and the bank is untouched
6000 syx build/host/bad.syx
6300 shot
6400 enc 6 -4
6600 shot
6700 keys 0x4880
7600 keys 0

# 7. save it again, this time with a name of its own: SELECT walks the letters, ALGORITHM sets one
7800 btn 9
7900 enc 6 15
8000 enc 1 -18
8100 enc 0 6
8200 enc 1 1
8300 shot
8400 btn 9

# 8. the same again, but backed out of partway: the letters typed are forgotten, and the sound that
# does get written later carries the name it already had
8700 btn 9
8800 enc 6 1
8900 enc 1 5
9000 btn 8
9100 shot
9200 btn 9
9300 enc 6 1
9400 btn 9
9600 shot

# 9. the host asks for the bank and for the edit buffer back
9800 syx build/host/req.syx
10100 shot

# 10. the other banks. SEL held, PRESETS goes a bank at a time: U1-21 to U2-01. A bank arriving
# now goes into U2, the bank you are in, and U1 is left as it was.
10300 btn 3 400
10400 enc 6 1
10800 shot
11000 syx build/host/bank.syx
11100 shot
11300 enc 6 -1
11400 enc 6 1

# 11. U2-01 is TEST 01 now. Save it to U3-05: SEL and PRESETS jump the slot two banks on, one
# back (to the start of U2), one on again, then four slots plain.
12400 btn 9
12500 btn 3 400
12600 enc 6 2
12700 enc 6 -1
12800 enc 6 1
13000 enc 6 4
13100 shot
13200 btn 9
13400 shot

# 12. the host asks again: the bank that comes back is U3, the one this voice is in
13500 syx build/host/req.syx

# 13. back with SEL: to the start of U3, to U2, and three more round to U8, the last
13800 btn 3 600
13900 enc 6 -1
14000 enc 6 -1
14100 enc 6 -3
14500 shot

14800 end
